# Generation lenses

Each Stage 2 generator works through exactly one lens. Different lenses approach the scope from different angles, so the candidate pool covers more ground and duplicates less. A generator may produce an idea that also fits another lens; that's fine, because dedupe handles it.

All lenses share one filter: the idea must exploit something current AI can do that existing solutions don't. Use `landscape.md` to find out what current AI can do, not your memory.

## labour — Labour cost
Find the expensive human work in the scope: skilled roles billed by the hour, outsourced services, backlogs, and work done overtime or by contractors.
- Search job postings, outsourcing marketplaces and salary data for the scope's roles. Postings show what work is being paid for today.
- Ask which tasks those people repeat, and what share of their hours goes to work current AI could draft, check or complete.
- Strong signals: a budget line already exists (an agency, a BPO, a consultancy), or the cost of delays is measurable.

## workflow — Value chain
Map the scope's workflow end to end: inputs, handoffs, approvals and outputs. Find the slow, error-prone or expensive steps.
- Look for steps where unstructured information (documents, email, calls, images) is turned into decisions or structured data by people.
- Look for handoffs where context is lost, and for reconciliation, checking and chasing work.
- Strong signals: the step gates revenue or compliance, and errors there are costly.

## capability — New capability unlock
Start from what AI can now do that it couldn't 12–18 months ago, according to `landscape.md`: for example agents and computer use, long context, vision, real-time voice, reasoning, cheaper inference. Ask where in the scope each one unlocks something that was impractical before.
- Prefer capabilities that crossed a usefulness threshold recently. Opportunities usually appear there before the incumbents move.
- For each idea, name the specific capability and the evidence that it now works.

## gap — Incumbent gap
Find where buyers are unhappy with existing tools and services in the scope.
- Search review sites, forums, community threads, social posts and complaints about incumbent products, including "<incumbent> alternative" queries.
- Look for categories still sold as services or with heavy implementation consulting, because software hasn't cracked them.
- Also check what incumbents have recently shipped in AI. A gap the incumbent closed last month isn't a gap.

## founder — Founder pain
Generate ideas from the user background in `scope.md`: problems this person has personally felt in their role and industry.
- Use their role to infer the recurring pain points, the buyers they know and the decisions they have seen made.
- If no background was given, use the most common practitioner roles in the scope instead, and note that this lens ran without user context.
